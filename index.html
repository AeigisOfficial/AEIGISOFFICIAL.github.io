AEIGIS

Sovereignty • Resilience • One Love

The Mainframe Crashed in Seattle. The Fortress Got Built in CNY.

In March 2026, a severe stroke left my left side paralyzed thousands of miles from home. When you cannot walk, the ground vanishes. The job goes. Your accounts hit negative digits. Your medical insurance cancels because COBRA is an unaffordable trap, leaving you with an $8,500 maximum out-of-pocket liability.

The heaviest weight wasn't the debt. It was navigating a system with limited help where the people in these fields are so often cold. It was the silence of sitting benched by reality, unable to send my daughter a single dollar while she packed her bags to start her freshman year of college.

But I never asked for charity. I am building my own door. I am dropping my art, my music, and my workbooks right now to raise my own money, stand back up on my own two feet, and build an independent brand that will eventually create real access and survival infrastructure for others stuck in the dark.

I am strictly pro-human. My maternal lineage carries the heavy history of Palestinian refugee exile, but my spirit belongs to universal human sovereignty. I am not here to play geopolitical games or pick a side in the grids of Palestine or Israel. One Love is not a cheap slogan; it is a structural necessity. We demand clean peace for every child of the soil, across every border, without compromise.

Welcome to the shield.
