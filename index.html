AEIGIS

Sovereignty • Resilience • One Love

The Mainframe Crashed in Seattle. The Fortress Is Building in Central New York.

In March 2026, a severe stroke left my left side paralyzed thousands of miles from home. I was living out in Seattle, working on the front lines as a Counselor, dedicating my life and my mind to giving back and holding up people who were broken. When the emergency hit, I was completely alone. I could barely walk unassisted and had to move back home to Central New York for direct family support.

I have done zero public fundraising or charity requests—I have just been quietly healing and trying to scrape by since my work stopped in March and my short-term disability insurance pay ended in June. Now, I am standing up to earn my living directly through my talents. People have been asking me to drop a full album for years, and I am putting my raw energy into answering that demand.

Due to physical recovery constraints, live performances are currently looking like they are about 18 months to 2 years out. But if the demand is there, and God clears me—which I know we will be strong through Faith—I am fully committed to a tour. Please like, support, share, email, and join our VIP fan club. Support my stroke recovery through these complications, and help me provide a platform to hopefully give back once I heal over the years.

My paternal lineage carries the heavy history of Palestinian refugee exile, but my spirit belongs to universal human sovereignty. We do not play geopolitical games or pick sides in corporate lines. One Love is not a cheap slogan; it is a structural necessity. We demand clean peace for every child of the soil, across every border, without compromise. Welcome to the shield.
