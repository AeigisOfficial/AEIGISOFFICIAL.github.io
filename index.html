The Mainframe Crashed in Seattle. The Fortress Is Building in Central New York.

In March 2026, a severe stroke left my left side paralyzed thousands of miles from home. I was living in Seattle, working on the front lines as a Counselor, dedicating my life and mind to holding space for people navigating deep personal transitions. When the emergency hit, I was completely alone. I could barely walk unassisted and moved back home to Central New York for family support.

I have done zero public fundraising or charity requests. I have simply been healing and scraping by since my work stopped in March and short-term disability ended in June. Now, I am earning my living directly through my talents by releasing the album people have asked for over the years.

Due to physical recovery constraints, live performances are roughly 18 months to 2 years out. If the demand is there and God clears me through Faith, I am fully committed to touring. Support my recovery, join the VIP fan club, and help build a platform to give back once I heal.

My paternal lineage carries the history of Palestinian refugee exile, and my spirit stands for universal human sovereignty. We reject geopolitical division. One Love is a structural necessity. Clean peace for every child of the soil across every border. Welcome to the shield.
